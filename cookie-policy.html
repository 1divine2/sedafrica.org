COOKIE POLICY

This Cookie Policy explains how Social and Economic Development for Africa uses cookies and similar technologies on its website. By continuing to browse our site, you consent to the use of cookies as described here.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and improve your browsing experience.

How we use cookies

We use cookies to understand how visitors interact with our site, improve page performance, and remember your preferences. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites or to sell your data.

Types of cookies we use

We may use essential cookies to make the site work, analytics cookies to understand how the site is used, and preference cookies to remember your settings. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. If you choose to block cookies, some parts of the website may not work as intended.
