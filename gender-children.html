PROGRAM 03 · GENDER & CHILDREN

SEDA empowers girls and young women with opportunities, confidence, and practical skills to participate fully in family, community, and national life.

“Every girl deserves the chance to stay in school and be heard.”

We support scholarships, skills acquisition, and the effective inclusion of girls and young women in decision-making and nation-building.

What this means in practice

Our work creates pathways for girls to remain in school and for young women to develop skills that can lead to self-employment. We also encourage families and communities to recognize their voices and leadership.

Why it matters

Removing barriers for women and girls changes the prospects of entire households. Participation, education, and economic confidence help build safer, fairer, and more capable communities.

  • Educational scholarships
  • Skills acquisition
  • Women in leadership
  • Child protection

THE WORK IN MOTION

Opportunity grows when every voice has room.

We create practical pathways for girls and young women to stay connected to education, skills, confidence, and community leadership.

Our commitment

Empowerment is more than a single opportunity. It is the steady work of removing barriers, building confidence, and making sure girls and young women can see themselves as contributors and leaders. SEDA works with families and communities to make those opportunities safer and more sustainable.

When a girl is supported, the benefit reaches beyond her own life. It strengthens her family, her peers, and the future of her community.
