PRIVACY POLICY

Social and Economic Development for Africa respects the privacy of everyone who interacts with us, whether through our website, programs, or partnerships. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

What we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, and any details you share with us through our contact form, volunteer application, or newsletter signup. We do not collect payment card details; those are processed securely by our donation partners.

How we use it

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiries, process your volunteer or newsletter registration, improve our programs, and keep you informed about our work. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Access to personal data is limited to SEDA staff who need it to carry out their responsibilities.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to request access to your information, correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can unsubscribe from our newsletter using the link in any email you receive from us.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at info@sedafrica.org or +232-76-920-000.
