Smallholder agriculture

PROGRAM 02 · AGRICULTURE

SEDA works with rural communities to make agriculture more sustainable, productive, and economically meaningful for smallholder farmers.

“A better harvest can change the future of a whole family.”

Our agriculture program promotes sustainable practices, increases rice production, and supports smallholder producers as they build stronger livelihoods.

What this means in practice

We help farming communities think beyond a single harvest by encouraging practical methods that protect soil, improve productivity, and strengthen household food security. Rice producers are central partners in shaping the work.

Why it matters

When smallholder farmers can produce more reliably and earn a stronger income, families are better able to meet daily needs, keep children in school, and invest in the future of their communities.

THE WORK IN MOTION

Food security begins with people who grow the food.

Our agriculture work connects knowledge, resources, and local leadership so farming families can plan beyond the next season.

Our commitment

SEDA's agriculture program treats farmers as partners and experts. We support approaches that respect local knowledge while opening access to better tools, stronger planning, and more resilient production. Rice remains an important focus because it is central to food security and household livelihoods.

With sustained collaboration, rural producers can build income, feed their families, and invest in a more secure future.
